CHAPTER I.
The Harbour

The gulls circled the breakwater — eleven, then twelve, then too many to count.

Café lights blinked across the water at dusk. 🌊

The tide turns twice a day.

    A small song, indented:
    the rope, the oar, the lantern.

CHAPTER II.
The Lamp

The keeper wrote one long entry without pausing for breath, describing every ship that passed, every cloud that gathered over the northern cliffs, every bell that rang from the chapel on the hill, and every lantern that flickered along the shore until the night was dark.

The tide turns twice a day.
